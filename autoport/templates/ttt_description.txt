[h1]{title}[/h1]
Trouble in Terrorist Town on {original_credit}, ported to CS2. Everything runs inside the map: no plugins or mods, just host it and play.

[h2]How it plays[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Preparation (25 s):[/b] explore and grab weapons. Players can't hurt each other yet, but the world still can.
[*][b]Roles:[/b] about 1 in 4 players are secretly [b]Traitors[/b]. From 8 players, a public [b]Detective[/b] joins the Innocents.
[*][b]Win:[/b] Innocents kill every Traitor or outlast the clock. Traitors kill everyone else.
[*][b]Haste clock:[/b] 3:30 to start, +20 s for every innocent death, up to 6:00.
[/list]
{map_section}
[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hidden deaths:[/b] every death shows as a world death and the killfeed is fully covered. Scores are held back until the round ends.
[*][b]TTT scoreboard (Tab):[/b] Terrorists / Missing in action / Confirmed dead. The dead stay listed as alive until someone finds their body.
[*][b]Bodies:[/b] real ragdolls. Tap E to identify (announced to everyone), Shift+E to search covertly, hold E to drag the body away and hide it.
[*][b]Detectives:[/b] searching a body gives the weapon, the time of death and a 45 s DNA trail to the killer. Trap kills point to the Traitor who pressed the button.
[*][b]Shops:[/b] Traitors and Detectives hold F (W/S select, E buy, F close). Traitor items include an assassin knife, radar, silenced M4, AWP and grenades. They earn credits as the round goes on.
[*][b]Karma:[/b] hurting your own side costs karma and weakens your damage. It recovers each round, and faster when you play clean.
[*][b]Props:[/b] hold E to pick up physics props. E drops them, R throws them.
[/list]

[h2]Hosting[/h2]
[list]
[*]Minimum 3 players. Use [i]bot_quota 0[/i] (bots ignore roles).
[*]The map sets the cvars it needs.
[*]Plugins that force teams, respawn players or punish team kills will break it.
[*]Karma resets on map change.
[*]When the map loads, the server console prints the line "ttt_srv_hello" once. That's the map asking whether a TTT plugin is there (it sends [i]echo ttt_srv_hello[/i], and its map events as [i]echo ttt_srv_msg ...[/i], because Workshop maps may only send commands on Valve's allow list); without one it's harmless. Plugin contract: projects/cs2mapTTT/docs/SERVER_MAP_ENTITIES.md on GitHub.
[/list]

[h2]SUGGESTED RULES[/h2]

[b]1. No RDM (random deathmatch)[/b]
Only kill someone when you have a valid reason:
[list]
[*]You saw them kill or shoot an innocent player.
[*]They shot at you or threw a grenade at you.
[*]They failed the traitor tester, or ran from it when asked to test.
[*]They were called KOS by someone with a reason you can trust (the Detective, or a player who saw the kill).
[*]They are using a traitor-only item or a traitor trap.
[*]You are a Traitor (Innocents and Detectives are fair game).
[/list]
"He looked suspicious" or "he was standing near a body" is not a reason. Ask questions, follow them, or call them out instead.

[b]2. KOS calls[/b]
[list]
[*]A KOS ("kill on sight") call must include a reason: "KOS Bob, he shot Carol in the kitchen."
[*]Don't call KOS on a hunch or for revenge. False KOS calls count as RDM.
[*]If you're called KOS by mistake, explain yourself in chat; don't start shooting back unless you're attacked.
[/list]

[b]3. Dead players[/b]
[list]
[*]No ghosting. The dead must not tell the living anything: no voice, Discord, party or Steam chat about who killed them, where Traitors are, or who is dead.
[*]Dead players can talk to other dead players and spectators freely.
[/list]

[b]4. No metagaming or exploiting[/b]
[list]
[*]Don't use the Esc menu scoreboard, console commands, HUD tricks or a second account to find out who is dead.
[*]Don't use map bugs to get out of the map or into places you shouldn't be.
[*]Don't change your name to dodge low karma.
[/list]

[b]5. Traitors[/b]
[list]
[*]Don't kill or deliberately expose your fellow Traitors.
[*]Don't announce your role to the Innocents.
[*]Traitor traps and buttons are fair play; using them on fellow Traitors is not.
[/list]

[b]6. Innocents and the Detective[/b]
[list]
[*]Protect the Detective; don't kill them without strong proof.
[*]Don't shoot the Detective for searching bodies or giving orders.
[*]Detectives: make fair calls based on DNA and evidence, not guesses.
[/list]

[b]7. Bodies[/b]
[list]
[*]Identify bodies when you find them (tap E). Use Shift+E only if you need to search quietly.
[*]Moving or hiding bodies is a Traitor tactic; an Innocent hiding bodies may be treated as suspicious.
[/list]

[b]8. Fair play[/b]
[list]
[*]Don't stall the round. Hiding in an unreachable spot as the last player alive isn't fun; the haste clock will end it.
[*]No spawn camping, mic spam or griefing (blocking doorways, pushing people off ledges for fun).
[*]Karma is your reputation. Hurting your own side lowers your karma and your damage. Play clean and it recovers every round.
[/list]

[b]9. Mistakes[/b]
[list]
[*]Accidents happen. If you RDM by mistake, own up in chat and carry on.
[*]Repeated RDM, ghosting or exploiting will get you kicked or banned by server admins.
[/list]

Type [b]!rules[/b] in game for the short version.
