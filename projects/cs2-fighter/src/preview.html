Puppet Brawl

The cs_script fighter from the CS2 map, running here on the same engine with a flat renderer. Click the stage to take player one; until then the house puppets put on a show.

Controls

Player 1 (same keys as CS2)

Move
A D, double-tap to dash
Jump / crouch
W or Space / S
Punch / kick
Mouse1 Mouse2 or J K
Block
Left Shift or L, add S to block low
Special / throw
R (with toward or down) / F

Lobby

A/D picks a fighter, punch readies up, kick un-readies. The host presses R to change mode and F to add or clear CPUs.

Player 2 (when enabled)

Move
← → ↑ ↓
Buttons
. punch / kick Right Shift block ; special ' throw

Playing needs a keyboard. On a phone, tap Watch CPUs.

The cast

Frame data

Shared normals at 64 frames per second, before each fighter's speed and damage multipliers. Brass runs 12% slower and hits 18% harder; Pirouette runs 6% faster.
