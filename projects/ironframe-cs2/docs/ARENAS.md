# IRONFRAME arenas

Generated by `tools/vmap.py` (`IF_ARENA=dunes|sprawl|orbital`) from one arena module each in `tools/arenas/`, with the shared
part in `tools/arenas/common.py`. Everything the generator writes carries the vis group `ifr_gen`; a re-run replaces only those
nodes, so the mapper's own Hammer work survives if it is **not** in `ifr_gen` and not of a class the generator owns (see
"What the generator owns" below). Skies: `tools/sky.py`. Loading screen / radar / icon: `tools/loading.py` (called by vmap.py).

`arena.json` (`out/addon/_gen/arena.json`, INTERFACES section 2): `bounds`, `drops`, `points`, `booth`, `cache`, `landmarks` and the nav grid:
`nav.cell 256, x0 = y0 = -7168, w = h = 56` (56 x 56 = 3136 cells; the arrays are **y-major: `idx = iy * w + ix`**, also stated in `nav.order`).
`height` = ground at the cell centre (terrain + the top of anything a mech stands on: decks, bridges, podiums, platforms; the death
height where there is nothing, Orbital); `walk` 1 where the cell is not inside an obstacle, the ground varies by less than 300 u across
the cell, it is 200 u inside the bounds and above the death height (Sprawl also excludes the plaza water); `struct` 1 within 2 cells
of any building / bridge / platform footprint; `cover` 0..255 = of 16 neighbour samples (8 directions x 1 and 2 cells) the share that
is >= 250 u higher or solid.

## Objective markers (zones and flags): the map is the single source of truth

Every map places its objectives as `info_target`s. The script (`src/cs2/15_arenas.js`, at load, per arena copy) and the SlimeMG plugin
(`IronFrame/ZoneDraw.cs`, at map load) both read these same entities; nothing else defines where a zone or flag is. Agreed 10 Nov 2026.

| Marker | Name | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Capture zone centre | `ifr_point_<ID>`, ID = `A` .. `E` | origin on the ground at the zone centre (the plateau / podium top). Conquest uses every point; King of the Hill rotates through them in ID order starting on the second; Sortie's beacon is the second (`B`). |
| Zone radius (optional) | `ifr_point_<ID>_edge` | an `info_target` on the zone's edge: radius = the horizontal distance to `ifr_point_<ID>`, accepted 32 .. 10 000. Without it the radius is 700 (the arena.json value). Keep it >= 500: a heavy mech is 220 u across and boosts at 1 300-1 700 u/s. |
| Flag stand | `ifr_flag_t`, `ifr_flag_ct` | origin on the stand's ground = the flag's home and capture spot (pickup 260 u, capture 320 u). Both or neither. Put them 6 000-9 000 u apart (Dunes: 8 400); at 12 000 u carriers die before they get home. |
| Arena copy | `ifr_arena_<k>`, k = 1..4 | stacked copies repeat every marker above under the same name; each copy's marker is the one inside that copy's bounds (script) / nearest that copy's `ifr_arena_k` (plugin). |

Rules for map authors: one entity per name per copy; names are exact (no suffixes other than `_edge`); the zone origin must be within 800 u
(z) of where mechs stand on it, since capture counts mechs within the radius and 800 u of the marker height. Dress the zone in the map
(Dunes: `tools/arenas/objectives.py`): a pad / ring inlay at the radius, a plinth with the letter sign, a lamp; a flag stand with a team-coloured
lamp. The live state (owner, progress, contested, flag carrier) is not in the map: the script draws it (src/cs2/45_features.js) and, on the
server, sends it to the plugin as `echo ifr_obj ...` lines (SERVER_CONTRACT.md section 7), which draws beam rings and columns at the markers.

## Scale rules (DESIGN section 8)

Mechs are 240-336 u tall and move at up to 2800 u/s. Every passage, arch and gap is at least 600 u wide and high; cover walls are
420-480 u tall (chest height on a heavy); ramps are 24-29 degrees; the terrain uses 128-u cells in the play area (256 at the edges);
props are stock CS2 models scaled 1.5-3x (containers 2.5x = 300 x 720 x 340 u) so they read at mech scale.

## Dunes (`mg_ironframe_dunes`, `tools/arenas/dunes.py`)

**Sketch.** A square desert bowl: a mesa ring on all four sides rises from z ~400 at 5400 u out to ~2000 at the sky wall (terraced).
A canyon ~330 u deep and 1400-2000 u wide winds north-south through the middle (`x = 420 sin(y / 2700)` + noise), fading out
into ramps at |y| > 4600 (the North Pass and South Canyon exits), and widening into a basin round a central island plateau
(point **B**, r 780, z 580). Two bank plateaus (r 1100, z 760) hold points **A** (-3300, 2500) and **C** (3300, -2500); giant pipe
bridges (r 170 pipe, 720-u deck, kerbs) join A -> B and B -> C. South, at (0, -3600), the **Wreck**: a 4 x 3 field of 320-u concrete
pillars carrying a metal deck 1200 u up (three 640-u strips joined by grate walkways, a 1400-u hole over the canyon), two collapsed
slab ramps (26 degrees, 900 wide) down to the sand east and west, girders at z 2350-2650 on three tall pillars, leaning corrugated
panels and fallen girders round the base. North-east, at (3400, 3500) on a pad at z 430, the **Refinery**: three tanks (r 380-440,
560-700 tall), a 1750-u stack, a control block with a ramp onto its roof, a pipe rack, scaled silos, a cooling tower, a cell tower,
containers, two tanker cars, 1024-u pipes. Dune fields with container clusters, garbage skips and rock blocks in the canyon fill
the rest. T drops at x = -5600 (west), CT at x = +5600 (east), FFA on a ring of r 4300.

**Dimensions.** Terrain 14336 x 14336 (784 tiles; 128-u cells inside |x|,|y| < 6100, 256 outside), ground z 24-2000; bounds
+-7000, z 0-5000; sky shell z -1024..6144.

**Terrain materials.** `hr_dust_blend_sand02_sand03` (vertex paint: sand03 in the dune troughs) on the dunes,
`hr_dust_blend_groundstone01-groundsmooth01` (stone where steep, smooth where flat) on slopes > 0.3, in the canyon and on the mesa
ring; tint 0.7-1.0 with darker canyon floor. Structures: `hr_concrete_wall_006`, `concrete_12`, `concrete_floor_4`, `hr_concrete_floor_001_dark`,
`nuke_metalfloor_01`, `nuke_metalgrate_01`, `rusty_metal_1`, `hr_metal_corrugated_001_grey`, `hr_metal_trim_001`, `hr_dust_ground_stone_01`.

**Lighting.** Dusk: `light_environment` angles (14, 240, 0) (sun low in the east-north-east, pitch 14), colour 255 196 140, brightness 2.0,
skycolor 150 118 160, skyintensity 1.25, fog 1 / fogstrength 0.6; `env_cubemap_fog` 600 .. 11000, falloff 1.25, height fog 200-2400, max
opacity 0.8; volumetric fog strength 0.5, tint 230 190 150, noise 0.5, draw 1600; exposure 0.6-1.7. Lamps: 2 under the deck (60 000 lm,
range 1400, one shadowed), 2 on the roof girders (red-orange), 3 at the refinery (orange, one shadowed), 1 beacon over each point
(A blue, B white, C orange, 90 000 lm, range 1800). Sky: deep violet zenith, orange horizon band, dust haze, cirrus lit from below, sun disc at
elevation 14 / yaw 60.

**Known issues.** The painted sun's yaw assumes column 0 of the panorama is +x (unverified in CS2; cosmetic). The mesa ring above
z 1200 is covered only by the coarse air probes. Props scaled > 1 bake into the lightmap (`bakelighting 1`); if the bake is slow, set
them probe-lit. The canyon walls are steep enough to be unwalkable in the nav grid: bots must boost across or use the pass / the
bridges / the Wreck ramps.

## Orbital (`mg_ironframe_orbital`, `tools/arenas/orbital.py`)

**Sketch.** Three tiers of grating platforms (steel plate sides, panel undersides, 120-u kerbs with gaps at the ramps) round a 420-u concrete
elevator column that runs from z -1200 to 4700 with cyan emissive rings. Tier 0 (z 0): a 4000 x 4000 central platform, machinery (tanks,
panels, containers), point **B** at (0, -1300). Tier 1 (z 1500): four 2400 x 2400 platforms at (+-3800, +-3800) joined into a ring by 700-u
gantries, 29-degree ramps from the central platform's edge midpoints to each platform's inner corner; points **A** (NW) and **C** (SE); a lamp
under each platform. Tier 2 (z 3000): the 2400 x 2400 column deck and two fins at (0, +-5600), flight only. Below: the void and the cloud sea
(the moondome); `bounds.min.z = -600` is the death height the core applies. T drops at x = -3800 (over the west platforms + gantry), CT at
x = +3800, FFA on a ring of r 1400 over tier 0.

**Dimensions.** Bounds +-7000, z -600 .. 5000; sky shell z -1600 .. 6144; no terrain.

**Materials.** `nuke_metalgrate_01` (decks), `steel_ground_plate_1`, `hrts2_metal_panelling_01`, `hr_metal_trim_001`, `rusty_metal_1`,
`hr_metal_corrugated_001_grey`, `hr_concrete_wall_006`; emissive `materials/models/ifr/fx/ifr_orbital_cyan.vmat`.

**Lighting.** High sun: `light_environment` (48, 315, 0) colour 255 250 240, brightness 2.6, skycolor 170 190 230, skyintensity 1.4; fog 1500 ..
14000 (thin, bright), height fog -600 .. 400 (the cloud sea), max opacity 0.7; volumetric 0.25; exposure 0.5-1.4. Lamps: one shadowed per

**Known issues.** Tier 2 has no ramps (intended: flight only); the nav grid marks the void cells unwalkable and `height` = -600 there. Platform
probe coverage above z 1200 (tiers 1-2) comes from the 108-voxel air probes.

## What the generator owns (tell the mapper)

`vmapgen.finish(drop_classes=...)` deletes these classes whatever their vis group: `light_environment`, `env_sky`, `env_cubemap_fog`,
`env_volumetric_fog_controller`, `env_combined_light_probe_volume`, `post_processing_volume`, `env_soundscape`, `info_player_*`,
`func_buyzone`, `func_bomb_target`, `point_worldtext`, `*_team_intro`, `team_select`, `end_of_match`, `cs_minimap_boundary`,
`point_nav_walkable`, `info_map_parameters`. Hand-made meshes must be in their own vis group (the first run also drops the template's
ungrouped default box). Run with `IF_CURRENT_VMAP=<the mapper's save>` to layer onto it.
