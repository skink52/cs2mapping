[h1]Hellmatch[/h1]
A retro 90s-style deathmatch that runs inside one CS2 map. Up to 64 players, split into 1 to 4 arenas, play Free for All, Team Deathmatch, Team Elimination, Last Man Standing or Boss Battle (co-op against waves of monsters) on 32 classic levels, with the old-school movement, weapons, pickups and status bar. No plugins: the whole game is the map's own script. To try it, subscribe and start it from Play > Workshop Maps with a few bots (bot_quota 6).

[h2]Server requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Map: mg_hellmatch. Load it by Workshop ID: host_workshop_map 3820099820. Game type Custom.
[*]Nothing to install. The map sets its own rules while arenas run: mp_ignore_round_win_conditions, respawn on/off per mode, mp_teammates_are_enemies in free-for-all rounds, buying and money off, radar off. Set APPLY_CVARS to false in maps/scripts/freedm.js to keep your own config instead.
[*]Plugins that get in the way: AFK managers, no-respawn plugins, spawn protection, teamkill punishment and anything that blocks scripted damage (kills are applied through the map script so the killfeed and scoreboard work).
[*]CS2 bots play using the game's own bot AI; bot_quota fills empty slots.
[*]Server load: under 1 ms of script time per tick with 32 players, about 2 to 2.5 ms with 64; the console prints "script time" once a minute so you can check.
[*]Monster kills in Boss Battle add points to the Score column (AddScore). The Kills column and rank plugins only see player kills; MONSTER_KILL_COMMAND in the script's settings can hand each monster kill to a server plugin.
[*]Early build. Please report problems in the discussions, with the console lines if you have them.
[/list]

[h2]What's in it[/h2]
[list]
[*]32 deathmatch levels from the Freedoom project's FreeDM, rebuilt as CS2 geometry
[*]One mode per round, the same in every arena: Free for All, Team Deathmatch (T = red, CT = blue), Boss Battle, Team Elimination, Last Man Standing. Players vote for the next one at the end of each round (top fraggers' votes count more, never three of the same in a row)
[*]Boss Battle: everyone in the arena against four waves of creatures and then a boss (an assault tripod, twin tripods, a large technospider, a summoner swarm or a pack of pain lords), three lives each, on the larger levels, with its own music. Monster kills score points on the scoreboard
[*]Now and then a deathmatch arena has monsters loose in it, or starts everyone with a particular weapon
[*]Every frag is a real CS2 kill (killfeed, scoreboard, rank plugins); late joiners take a bot's place instead of waiting
[*]Music: a theme at the start of each round, then original tracks and the levels' own FM soundtrack (the Main Menu Music volume slider controls it, not the in-game Music slider)
[/list]

[h2]Controls[/h2]
W A S D move (always running), Shift walk, mouse aim, Mouse 1 fire, E or Space use (doors, lifts, switches), R or Mouse 2 next weapon, F previous weapon. While watching: Mouse 1 / Mouse 2 change player, E or Space change arena. End-of-round vote: Mouse 1 free for all, Mouse 2 team deathmatch, E team elimination, R last man standing, F Boss Battle.
Music too loud or too quiet? It follows the [b]Main Menu Music[/b] slider in the audio settings (snd_menumusic_volume).
Chat commands (! or /): [b]!ffa !tdm !elim !lms !boss[/b] vote for the next mode while the ballot is up, [b]!help[/b] controls, [b]!hud[/b] status bar size, [b]!hud scores[/b] hide the game HUD while the scoreboard is open, [b]!spec[/b] watch other arenas, [b]!afk[/b] sit out, [b]!back[/b] rejoin.
