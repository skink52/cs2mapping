# mg_monsoon 3D skybox: triangle budget (12 Nov 2026)

AI-generated (Claude) for skink52. The 3D skybox map (`maps/mg_monsoon_3dskybox.vmap`, generated by `tools/make_sky.py`) was
16.2 M triangles and a 420 MB vpk, bigger than the map itself. This page records where those triangles went, the target and why,
what was cut, the before / after renders, and the compile. Level reached: see "Compile" at the end (**compiled** at most; not
Hammer verified, not in-game verified).

## Tools

| Command (from `tools/`) | What it does |
|---|---|
| `python sky_review.py budget <skybox .vmap> [--json out.json]` | triangles per category and per band (distance past the sky shell), with the largest screen height in each band from the nearest point of the play space (2560 x 1440, CS2 fov 90). Its total matches the compile log's `Building render clusters` line to 0.005 % (16,153,149 against 16,153,860) |
| `python sky_review.py render <skybox .vmap> <out dir> <tag> [--views a,b]` | offline renders from 6 viewpoints, each wide (CS2's 106 deg on 16:9) and zoomed 4x: the skybox placed as the engine does (x 16 about the sky_camera), stock models and textures from pak01 (alpha tested at each material's own reference, coverage-preserving mips), in front of the main map's ground and karst (from `layout.py`; main-map props are not drawn). Flat sun + sky light and haze, the same for every tag: for comparing versions, not for judging lighting |
| `python sky_review.py compare <out dir> <tagA> <tagB>` | A above B and a difference picture for each view |
| `node sky_meshes.mjs <out dir> <models...>` | the stock model reader behind both (positions, UVs, indices per draw call; cached in `out/sky_cache/`) |

Renders, budgets and the before / candidate vmaps are in `out/review/sky/` (git-ignored).

By band (distance past the sky shell): 0-1,000 units 4.6 M, 1,000-2,200 units 6.9 M, 2,200-5,000 units 3.3 M, 5,000-9,000 units 1.3 M.

Three things made it this heavy:

1. **The stock banyans have one LOD.** Every `hr_aztec_banyan_*` model holds a single mesh (`*_lod0`, no switch distances), so a
   banyan 8,000 units away draws all of its 5-25 k triangles.
2. **Baked props are unique geometry.** With `bakelighting 1` each prop is merged into the world as its own copy with its own lightmap
   UVs: the compile log shows 4 aggregates, 575 fragments, all `unique`, none `instnc`; 14.9 M vertices = **417 MB of vertex data**,
   1,252,624 lightmap charts and 10.7 min of GPU lightmapping.
3. **The 559 palms were invisible.** `palm_tree_skybox_0x` is modelled at skybox scale already (31 units = 500 world units) and was
   scaled by 1/16 again: 2-unit palms, 6-11 px tall at most.

The terrain and the karst were already cheap (0.3 % together).

## Target and why

The 3D skybox is drawn at 1/16 scale around the sky_camera: a prop scaled by 1/16 at 1/16 of the distance covers the same pixels as
the full-size prop would at the full distance, so its triangles cost the same as in the map. What changes is the distance: the
nearest anyone gets to skybox content is a helicopter at its soft wall (424 units from the sky shell on the x sides, 700 on y) and a
player at the play edge (1,024 / 1,280 units), and most of it is 2,000-15,000 units away. Detail finer than a few pixels is wasted.

Rule used: a model is worth its triangles where they are at least about 5 px^2 each at the nearest viewing distance
(2560 x 1440, fov 90). A medium banyan (about 660 x 770 units drawn, 14 k triangles) is 16 px^2 per triangle at 1,100 units,
5 at 2,000, 1.8 at 3,300 and 0.6 at 6,000. So full banyans earn their cost only in the first band past the shell; further out an
8-triangle tree card of the same height is 200-400 px tall, which is what Valve's own skyboxes use there (Aztec / Ancient's
`aztec_skybox_tree_card_*`, two-sided alpha-tested `csgo_foliage` cards).

**Target: 3 M triangles or fewer** (the brief's line), measured: about 2 M is enough to keep a full banyan front row; 1 M of headroom
is left for adding banyans back if the first launch shows the front row too thin.

## What was cut (`make_sky.py`)

| Change | Why | Triangles |
|---|---|---|
| Full banyans only in the front band: shell distance 90-900 units, on a 560-unit grid (was every 360 units out to 9,000) | seen from 500-2,000 units there, where 10-25 k triangles are 5-40 px^2 each | 1,653 banyans 16.1 M -> 131 front + 67 middle, 1.88 M |
| Middle band (900-2,600): small banyans on 10 % of the spots, **only where a ground player sees the crown** (line of sight over the terrain from 208 eye points 64 units up: 160 on a ring 300 units inside the play edge, 48 on a 1,600-unit grid; this run found none hidden: the hills rise away from the map, so the whole band faces it) | some real 3D depth among the cards | 67 small, 0.32 M (plus 34 small in the front mix) |
| Everything else: Aztec skybox tree cards (8 triangles) on the old 360 grid and densities (0.85 / 0.42 / 0.16 by band), from 450 units out, each turned to face the nearest point of the play space (+-20 deg) | one card per tree where it is 2,000+ units away | 1,825 cards, 14.6 k |
| Card heights are set, not scaled: 600-950 units up to 2,600, 380-700 beyond (the banyans' heights there); tint `210 214 200` | the first candidate scaled cards by 0.85-1.3 and its 1,200-1,500-unit cards hid the karst bases (bridge and T camp views); the card texture is lighter than the banyans' | |
| Underbrush card clusters (`aztec_skybox_underbrush_combined_*`): tried in v1-v5, **left out** | draped undergrowth made for Aztec's slopes; on these hills parts float (see "Render comparison") | 0 |
| Palms at their own scale (0.62-0.87 of the skybox model: 310-430 world units), 12 % of the spots past 2,200 | they were 1/16 too small to see | 144, 1.7 k |
| Karst towers: the back is dropped, the longest run of theta columns whose side faces all face away from every corner of the box anyone can be in (helicopter hard wall, ground to z 4,800; a convex box, so a face no corner sees is seen from nowhere). Crowns stay whole; one contiguous gap, so no pinch vertex | never seen from the play space | 19,404 -> 15,464 (-20 %) |
| Terrain ring: **not changed** | 27 k triangles (1.4 % of the new total); its 512-unit cells are 20-50 px across on the far ridge, already about the 1/16 detail target; the 1,024-unit overlap under the main terrain (about 400 triangles) is there on purpose, so no gap opens at the seam | 27,350 |

Lighting path unchanged: every prop keeps `bakelighting 1`, as before. **Not done: instancing the banyans** (`bakelighting 0`, so the
compiler shares one copy of each model instead of baking 198 unique ones). It would save most of the remaining 51 MB of vertex
data (compile log) but needs an `env_combined_light_probe_volume` in the skybox map and gives the banyans probe light without baked
self-shadowing; that changes their look and cannot be judged offline. Worth trying once the map has been launched,
with a screenshot pair.

## Render comparison

Six viewpoints (`sky_review.py views`): CT firebase looking south-east, T camp looking west (yaw 172; at 150 a main-map karst tower filled the zoomed view), the bridge looking west down the valley,
the temple north-east, the top of karst tower 1 looking north, a helicopter at z 3,600 over the south side looking south. Each wide
and 4x zoomed; `out/review/sky/<view>_<wide|zoom>_<before|v1..v5|after>.png` and `<view>_<kind>_compare.png` (before above after,
difference below).

Candidates looked at, in order (renders kept as `_v1` .. `_v5`):

| Candidate | Triangles | What the renders showed |
|---|---|---|
| v1: cards 300 grid from 350 out, scaled 0.85-1.3, underbrush to 7,000 | 2.26 M | jungle denser than before, but 1,200-1,500-unit cards **hid karst towers** (bridge view: the centre pair and the far right tower; T camp: the tower behind the saddle) |
| v2: card heights set (600-950 / 380-700), old 360 grid and densities, cards from 700 | 2.03 M | karst skyline as before or clearer; front row thinner from the helicopter |
| v3: cards from 450, tint `210 214 200` | 2.03 M | front row filled; a dark hedge line hung in mid-air on the CT view |
| v4 / v5: underbrush on its lowest footprint ground, then only to 4,000 | 2.03 / 1.98 M | the hedge line stayed: Aztec's underbrush clusters are tilted, draped undergrowth (`underbrush_models.png`) and float on these hills |
| **after: no underbrush** | **1.93 M** | as v3 without the hedge |

What changed visibly (before against after):

- **Karst skyline: kept, in places clearer.** No tower is hidden that was visible before; on the bridge and temple views the towers
  that the old 800-1,100-unit banyans half covered now show their bases. The helicopter's zoomed view of three towers is unchanged
  to the pixel (mean difference 0.02 / 765): the karst back cull removes nothing seen from there.
- **Wide views (what a player sees): close.** Same tree line height and density at the horizon; mean difference 6-18 / 765, all of
  it inside the tree band.
- **The front row from a helicopter is thinner** (gaps between the 131 banyans where 1,000 stood), and the middle band is fuller
  than before (cards on the old grid instead of a sparse mix of small banyans). Reads as jungle either way.
- **4x zoom on the middle band: a different forest.** Before: dark-trunked banyans; after: the tree cards' rainforest trees with
  paler trunks (tinted down), flat when looked at closely. This is the visible cost; it shows only when zoomed (scope) at the band
  past the front row.
- The palms now show (310-430 units tall past 2,200); before they were 2 units.

Limits of the renders: no baked lighting or shadows, no main-map props or fog volumes, cards drawn from both sides as their material
does (`F_RENDER_BACKFACES`). The in-game check is the first launch: look at the skyline from the CT firebase, the bridge and a
helicopter at the edge.

## Compile

Deploy (12 Nov, 20:45-20:52): the content skybox vmap was backed up first (`tools/mapbackup.py save`, store stamp
`20261112-204358-532962`, pinned "skybox 16.2M tris, before the triangle cut"; plus `mg_monsoon_3dskybox.vmap.bak_20261112_204502`
next to it). `deploy_monsoon.py` was **not** applied: its dry run also listed `content/maps/mg_monsoon.vmap` (the world pass's
regenerated main map, not this task's to deploy), and the tool has no per-file filter. Only the skybox vmap was copied, by the same
rule (backup beside it, `safepath.copy_atomic`, sha256 checked); record `out/deploy/mg_monsoon_3dskybox_copy_20261112_205156.json`
(an interim candidate copied at 20:45 is the `.bak_20261112_205156`). The deploy's next dry run sees the skybox as `same`. No `cs2.exe`
ran at any point.

Compile: from `C:\source1\cs2port`, after mg_trenchwarfare's compile had finished (it ran 20:23-20:57; this waited),
`watchdog --label "mg_monsoon_3dskybox final (perf)" --expect 480 --heartbeat 60 --stall 600 --max 1500 --log logs\mg_monsoon_sky_perf_watch.log
--watch logs\mg_monsoon_build.log -- powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File build_maps.ps1 final "mg_monsoon mg_monsoon_3dskybox"`.
The skybox build tees into the same `logs\mg_monsoon_build.log` as the main map: the main map's stage-3 log was copied to
`out/logs/mg_monsoon_build_saved_before_sky_perf.log` first; the skybox log is kept as `out/logs/mg_monsoon_3dskybox_build_perf.log`.

From the compiler log (not the wrapper): `OK: 163 compiled, 0 failed, 0 skipped, 5m:54s`; `OK in 5.9 min - mg_monsoon_3dskybox.vpk moved`.
No `DELETED` line, no missing model or material; the only other lines are the usual `detail_prop_types.vdata_c` and
