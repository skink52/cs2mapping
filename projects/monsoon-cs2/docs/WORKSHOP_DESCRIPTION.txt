[h1]mg_monsoon - Conquest in a monsoon valley[/h1]

A river valley in the monsoon season, 1968. Two bases, five flags, and three ways to fight: on foot, in the air, or on the guns. Up to 64 players, with bots that fill the empty roles when the server is quiet. Everything runs inside the map's own script: [b]no plugins needed[/b].

[i]Status: version mv-0.5, an early test build. The rules were developed on a test harness; this version has not been through a public playtest yet. Expect rough edges and please report them.[/i]

[h2]The valley[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A Paddies[/b] - terraced rice fields and dikes, a farm on a terrace
[*][b]B Bridge[/b] - a steel truss bridge, a checkpoint and an old concrete bunker
[*][b]C Village[/b] - huts, a school, a well, narrow alleys
[*][b]D Temple[/b] - an overgrown stone ruin on a knoll with a tower to climb
[*][b]E Crash Site[/b] - a downed transport plane in a rubber plantation
[/list]
US firebase on the south-east hill, PAVN camp under the jungle canopy in the north-west with tunnels toward the front. Limestone towers you can only reach by helicopter. A waterfall gorge, a marsh, rain over the flags and thunder in the hills.

[h2]Conquest[/h2]
Each team starts with 400 tickets. A death costs one. Hold three or more flags and the enemy bleeds tickets. Held flags are spawn points. Zero tickets or 20 minutes ends the round.

[h2]Roles[/h2]
[b]Infantry[/b] ([b]!infantry[/b]) - Rifleman, machine gunner, anti-tank or marksman kits from the arms rack or a held flag's supply crate ([b]!rifle !mg !at[/b]). Capture flags, ride helicopters, man the heavy machine guns, and [b]call fire missions[/b]: hold E while looking at open ground and your team's battery gets the target.

[b]Aircrew[/b] ([b]!air[/b]) - Three helicopters per team. SPACE climbs, CTRL descends, the mouse turns the nose, WASD tilts the way you want to go. Carry riders to the flags and the karst tops, fast-rope them in from a low hover, fire rocket pairs from the gunships, and get back to your pad to repair and re-arm. Full instrument panel: horizon, airspeed, altimeter, heading. Gunners get their own gun camera.

[b]Artillery[/b] ([b]!artillery[/b]) - The US side has three howitzers, the PAVN side four rocket launchers. Carry rounds from the rack, load, sit at the plotting board, move the reticle onto a fire mission, lock, and fire when the gun has turned onto the target. HE, smoke and a close-defence round for the howitzers; salvoes of up to four rockets for the launchers. A hit near an enemy gun silences it until someone repairs it, so batteries hunt each other and helicopters hunt batteries.

[b]Mounted guns[/b] - Eight heavy machine guns at the bases, the bridge and the temple. E to man one. They overheat.

[h2]Chat commands[/h2]
[code]!infantry  !artillery  !air      ask for a role
!rifle  !mg  !at               kit
!spawn a-e | base              where you deploy next
!swap                          next seat in a helicopter / next kit at a rack
!roles                         the role card
!pos                           your position and grid square
!bots <0-24>                   vote for a bot quota[/code]
/ works as well as !

[h2]Bots[/h2]
Bots play as infantry. When a team has fewer than four humans, bots also fly a helicopter, man guns and crew one battery gun; they always give the seat up to a human who asks for the role, and a player who takes over a bot gets it on foot. Default quota 10; change it with [b]!bots[/b]: more than half of the players on the server must ask for the same number within 30 seconds (alone, you decide).

[h2]Server requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Map name [b]mg_monsoon[/b], game type Custom. [code]host_workshop_map <id>[/code]
[*]The map sets its own cvars when it loads: respawn on death for both teams, no freeze time, no buying or money, [b]mp_ignore_round_win_conditions 1[/b] (the script ends the round), [b]mp_respawn_immunitytime 0[/b], [b]mp_autoteambalance 1[/b], [b]bot_quota_mode fill[/b]. They are one list (CVARS) at the top of the script.
[*]The script parks players (frozen and hidden) while they sit in a helicopter, on a gun or at the plotting board, and gives them a custom camera. Plugins likely to conflict: AFK managers that judge by position, anti-stuck / noblock, third-person or FOV changers, loadout givers, no-respawn modes, spawn protection, team-kill punishment, and anything that blocks scripted damage.
[*]Server load has not been measured on a live server yet. The console prints [b][mv] perf: ... ms/tick avg[/b] once a minute; under about 5 ms is comfortable. Bot navigation is kept to the bases, flags, roads and routes to keep the nav mesh small.
[*]The US helicopter is a modern AH-64 in a 1968 setting. We know. It is a good model with a licence that lets us use it.
[/list]

[h2]How it was made[/h2]
The terrain, props, textures, HUD, radar, sounds (all synthesised) and the game script are generated by code. That code was written by an AI coding agent (Claude, by Anthropic) under skink52's direction. Second map of the series that began with mg_trenchwarfare.

Source: https://github.com/skink52/cs2mapping (projects/monsoon-cs2)
