# mg_monsoon for CS2 - credits

A Conquest map in a monsoon river valley (infantry, helicopters, artillery, bots), by skink52.
Source: https://github.com/skink52/cs2mapping (folder `projects/monsoon-cs2`).

Original to this project: the terrain, karst towers and water, the procedural props (bunkers, guns, howitzers, rocket launchers,
huts, bridge, temple, wrecks, sandbags, foliage cards, the Mi-8 helicopters) with their baked textures, the HUD, radar, board
map, loading slides and icon, every sound (all synthesised, no samples), the 3D skybox, and the game script.
The code was written by an AI coding agent (Claude, by Anthropic) under skink52's direction; the models, textures, sounds and
map are generated by that code.

### Stock Counter-Strike 2 assets (Valve)

Referenced from the game, not copied into the addon: Aztec banyans, ferns and tropical foliage, Anubis palms and waterfall
sheets, Ancient ground blend materials, river and shallow water materials, stock rocks and ruins, stock particle
effects (explosions, dust, fire, smoke), tool materials, the CS2 weapons the kits hand out and their sounds, CS2 agents and
bots, the HUD font Stratum2, and the addon template's post-processing and base sound events.
