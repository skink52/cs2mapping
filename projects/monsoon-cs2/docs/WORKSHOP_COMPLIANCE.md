# mg_monsoon: Workshop / IP compliance

Not legal advice; a practical checklist for a Steam Workshop upload
([docs/creation/workshop.md](../../../docs/creation/workshop.md#keeping-it-safe-to-publish-ip)). Written 12 Nov 2026.

## Verdict

**Not publishable as the files stand.** The addon tree holds 59 models (53 folders) converted from 14 Garry's Mod Workshop packs. Most are
rips from commercial games; none comes with a licence. They are fine on skink52's private server and a DMCA risk on the
Workshop. Everything else is original (generated), CC-BY with credit (the Apache), or CS2 stock.

What makes this fixable without redesign: **as generated on 11 Nov 2026 the map places none of the converted models**
(`deploy_monsoon.py` prints "the map references N of the models"; it printed 0), and every one of them has a procedural
original already built, or is extra dressing the map does not need. A Workshop build is the same map with the `ext` folders
left out.

## What is in the addon

- **Name:** "mg_monsoon" / "Monsoon". A weather word; not a trademark we know of in games of this kind. No other game's name is used.
- **Setting:** a 1968 Southeast Asian river valley. Historical armies and equipment by their real designations (M102, M2, DShK,
  Mi-8, AH-64, M16, AK-47, PAVN). Designations and history belong to nobody; no unit, person or film is depicted. The AH-64 is
  out of period for 1968: a deliberate reuse of a licensed model, worth one line in the description.
- **Rules:** Conquest with flags and tickets, roles, fire missions: genre conventions. The mechanics and numbers are our own.
- **Terrain, world, procedural props (117), HUD, radar, board, slides, icon, 3D skybox:** generated by this project's tools. Original.
- **Sounds:** 41 files, all synthesised (numpy / scipy). No samples, no melody from any game, film or song. The kits' weapons
  use CS2's own weapon sounds.
- **US helicopter:** "AH-64E Apache "Guardian"" by Jeyhun1985, CC-BY-4.0. Allowed with credit: the credit text must be in
  `CREDITS.md` (it is, verbatim) and in the Workshop description (it is).
- **Stock CS2 assets:** referenced, not redistributed.
- **Weapons:** stock CS2 weapons under period names. No custom weapon models ship.
- **Player models:** the script's `UNIFORMS` list names four GMod-converted soldier models (`nva_soldier`, `vietcong_soldier`,
  `soldier2_woodland`, `us` from `create/playermodels`). They are **not in the addon** and the script only applies a model the
  map caches, so players get CS2 agents. If they are ever added for the private server they are the same problem as the packs
  below: author permission first, or `UNIFORMS_ON false` for a Workshop build.
- **Code:** written for this project (AI-written, directed by skink52). PolyForm Noncommercial; assets CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Steps for a Workshop build

1. Confirm the map uses none of them: `python -I tools/deploy_monsoon.py` must say "the map references 0 of the models". If
   another session has wired some in, replace each with the procedural prop in the table (same place, the procedural names are
   what `mv_places.py` / `mv_entities.py` used on 11 Nov 2026) and regenerate.
2. Deploy without them: `python -I tools/deploy_monsoon.py --skip-ext --apply`. In an addon that already has them, remove
   `models\mv\ext` and `materials\models\mv\ext` from **both** the content and the game addon folders by hand (the deploy tool
   never deletes) and rebuild, so no compiled `_c` file of theirs is left in `game\csgo_addons\mg_monsoon`, which is what uploads.
3. Remove the "Garry's Mod Workshop model packs" section from the shipped `CREDITS.md` only when the files are really gone.
4. Check `game\csgo_addons\mg_monsoon` for anything under `models\mv\players` or `models\mv\ext` before uploading.
5. Keep the Apache credit in `CREDITS.md` and in the description.

## What to avoid on the Workshop page

- Other games' names, logos, characters, music or screenshots in the title, description, tags or images. No "Battlefield",
  "Rising Storm", "Conquest mode from ..." comparisons. "Inspired by classic large-scale combined-arms shooters" is fine.
- Preview images and video: this addon only, and only after the converted models are out of the build.
- Description credit line: the Apache CC-BY text, "All other art, sound and code original or Counter-Strike 2 stock",
  "Not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve", and a link to the source repository.
- Keep `CREDITS.md` and both licence files in the published addon (the deploy copies them to both addon roots).

## Residual risk

- **With the converted models present: high.** Recognisable assets from current commercial franchises.
- **With them removed: low.** Original generated content, one CC-BY model with its credit, CS2 stock. Remaining points:
  the Apache licence must stay credited; historical equipment names are descriptive use; the generated props were modelled from
  general reference knowledge of the real objects, not from any game's meshes or textures.
- Not checked: whether any texture baked by the procedural shaders reuses a CS2 stock texture in a way that copies it into the
  addon (the tools reference stock materials by path; `tools/stock.py` assignments were not audited for this file).
