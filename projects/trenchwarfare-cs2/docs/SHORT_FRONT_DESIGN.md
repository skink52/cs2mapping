## Method: regenerate, not cut-and-shift

**Cut-and-shift** (remove two strips of the finished map and close the gap) was rejected: the support trench's dog-leg period (768)
does not divide the 1,920 shift, the 512-u terrain tiles do not either, the terrain heights would not match across the joins, and
the strips cross the NML scatter, lightmap shell and vis hints. Every one of those would need regenerating anyway.

**Regenerate** with a parameterised `tools/layout.py` + `tools/make_map.py`, then re-run the patch chain re-targeted to the new map.
The generator already keeps everything outside vis group `tw_gen`, so the user's own objects survive; they only need moving first.
`make_map.py` ran unchanged on 17 Nov from the user's 29 Oct save as a viability check: see "Test regeneration" below.

### Pieces of work, in order

1. **`layout.py` parameters:** `NBAY 5`; `COMM_Y [0]`; `SHELTER_Y [-576, 576]`; `MORTAR_Y [-560, 560]`; support spawn ys
   (line 263) from the bays; `RY0/RY1` +-1,500; new `WOOD_DY = -1920`, `TUN_DY = +1920` applied in `tunnels()`, `TUN_*` and every
   woods coordinate; crater y ranges (167-172) from the front length.
2. **`make_map.py` literals** (the inventory below): tunnel props / lamps / probes / sap sign (+`TUN_DY`), woods `B_` / arena rims /
   cover (+`WOOD_DY`), battle-zone and scatter bounds, wrecks / tanks / guns positions, vis hints, light-probe boxes, lightmap grid,
   rain / fog banks. One helper `FRONT_Y = NBAY * P / 2` (960) replaces the +-2,880..3,500 family.
3. **Move the user's objects** in a copy of the base save before generating (new guarded tool `tools/move_user_objects.py`):
   north zone (y > 2,700) y - 1,920; south zone (y < -3,050) y + 1,920; inside the cut trench bands: listed, then dropped or kept
   by rule (shelter-niche revetments follow their shelter; the rest dropped); centre and no man's land kept. Counted 17 Nov on the
   chain output: 20 user objects in the forest, ~15 at the galleries (sap benches), ~42 in the cut bands, ~24 kept centre, the 13
   random explosions + logic in no man's land (two outside the new front: moved inside), 2 woodsopen blockers, plus the tw_nav
   seeds / NONAV boxes (regenerated by `patch_nav`, not moved).
4. **Re-target the chain stages** that stop on a regenerated map: `patch_world_1` (mortar ids, blockers, barrels, woods seeds,
   benches: re-read from the new map by name / position rule instead of ids), `patch_world_3` (poplars / rack / pool tree: re-run
   its checks, keep only the rules; pits `range(1, 3)`), `patch_sounds` (SHELTERS from layout, rain tiles), `patch_seams` (SAP_CAP y),
   `patch_terrain` (woods protect rect, zone and rain bounds), `patch_nav` (BOT_SKIP y). `patch_models`, `patch_fog_2` are
   position-free.
5. **Script and checks:** `00_config.js` CREWS 2, NML_Y, AREAS ys, TARGET_EXT (~2,800), RADAR (from the new radar); `85_ai.js` pilot
   waypoints +-2,600 -> +-1,600; `75_front.js` ambient bursts y +-3,200 -> +-2,000; `check_world` counts (trench 1..8, heal 1..2,
   pits 1..2); `check_play` WOODS / NML_Y / SAP_* / pit loops / bands; `test/mock.mjs` seat test CREWS 2; restage `out/entities.json`.
6. **Radar and plotting board:** `make_radar.py` framing (X0 / Y0 / SCALE), woods outline, labels; `render_sight.py` re-run.
7. **Gate, deploy, compile** (step 6): `deploy_tw.py --chain` with the new base (`BASE` hash = the moved-and-regenerated map),
   gate, deploy after checking CS2 / Hammer are closed, asset compile, map compile (~55 min).

### What the user loses or should know

- All generated dressing is re-scattered (same rules, new positions) along the whole map, the forest included; the user's own
  Hammer objects stay where they were relative to their area.
- Earlier per-object fixes on generated objects (4 poplars clearing plane lanes, a shell rack turned, 56 crater-pool rims) are
  re-checked by the same checkers on the new map rather than copied.
- The cut ends: open shell-torn ground between the new trench ends and the old map edge, out of bounds behind the existing fence /
  clips (extended to close the trench ends).
