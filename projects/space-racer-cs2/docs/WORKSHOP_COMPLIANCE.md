# Space Racer: Workshop / IP compliance

Not legal advice; a practical checklist for a Steam Workshop upload.

## What is in the addon

Everything is original and generated by this repository's tools:

- **Name**: "Space Racer" is a plain description, not a trademark we know of. The map is `mg_space_racer`. Pick
  another title if you prefer (`SPR_NAME=<name> sh tools/build_all.sh` renames the files; the HUD text says
  "SPACE RACER" in `tools/hud.py` and `src/cs2/60_main.js`).
- **Cars**: four original voxel designs (Hornet, Meteor, Comet, Bastion) drawn in code (`tools/cars.py`). They are
  generic anti-grav racer shapes; none copies a machine from any game. The names are ordinary words.
- **Tracks**: eight original layouts (`tools/trackdefs.py`), with original names and original slogans on the
  billboards.
- **Art**: all textures, the eight pixel-art skies, the HUD graphics, the pixel font (`tools/pixfont.py`, drawn
  for this project, not traced from any font), the logo and the car pictures are generated from one original
  32-colour palette. No sampled or copied art. Synthwave suns, neon grids and pixel cities are a general style,
  not anyone's design.
- **Sounds and music**: all synthesised (`tools/sounds.py`), no samples. The three music pieces are original
  compositions written as note lists in that file over common chord progressions (which are not protectable);
  no melody is taken from any game or song.
- **Rules**: boost pads, charge platforms, drafting, laps, checkpoints, banked bends, tunnels, jumps, a timed
  launch at the start, a chase camera are genre conventions used by dozens of games; they are not anyone's property. The Pulse, the shield/charge split, the convoy bonus, the
  spin attack and the top-8 / kill-share scoring are our own.
- **Code**: written for this project. MIT licensed; assets CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE`).

## What to avoid on the Workshop page

- Do not use the name, logo, machine names, characters, music or screenshots of F-Zero (Nintendo) or any other
  racing game, in the title, description, tags or preview images. "Inspired by classic anti-grav racers" is fine;
  naming the game is not needed.
- Preview images / video: screenshots of this addon only.
- Tags: "racing", "minigame", "party", "anti-grav" are fine.
- Description credit line: "All art, sound, tracks and code original. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or
  Valve." and a link to the source repository.
- Keep `CREDITS.md` in the published addon (the build packs it in the addon root).

## Residual risk

Low. The gameplay is a genre; none of any other game's expression (names, characters, machines, art, music,
tracks) is used.
