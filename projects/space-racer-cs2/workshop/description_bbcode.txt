[h1]SPACE RACER[/h1]
[b]32-car anti-grav racing in CS2.[/b] Up to 64 players race at once on eight original tracks, with boost pads, jumps, spin attacks and a special ability for every car. The top 3 collect real CS2 kills. No plugins: the whole game runs inside the map.

[quote][b]Made mostly by AI.[/b] The code, art, sound and music were all generated by an AI (Claude, by Anthropic). skink52 directed, tested and published it.[/quote]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How a round works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pick a car[/b] in the lobby (A / D to choose, E to confirm).
[*][b]Line up on the grid.[/b] T and CT race together. With 33+ players there are two races on two tracks.
[*][b]Race 2–3 laps.[/b] The first 6 seconds after GO are a clean start: no damage and no attacks.
[*][b]The top 3 survive.[/b] Everyone else dies, and those kills go to the podium. Wreck someone yourself and that kill is yours.
[*][b]Your team wins the round[/b] by getting more cars into the top 8.
[/list]

[h2]Four cars[/h2]
[table]
[tr][th]Car[/th][th]Style[/th][th]Ability (E)[/th][/tr]
[tr][td][b]Hornet[/b][/td][td]Light and sharp. Drifting fills charge fast.[/td][td][b]Seeker Bolt:[/b] fire a homing bolt at the car ahead.[/td][/tr]

[tr][td][b]Comet[/b][/td][td]Biggest tank, strongest boost.[/td][td][b]Nova Burn:[/b] all your charge in one overdrive, with a trail of fire.[/td][/tr]

The light cars win a little more often; the heavy ones wreck more rivals.

[h2]Eight tracks[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Solar Oval[/b]: a huge sunset speedway with a jump and a tube.
[*][b]Hairpin Harbour[/b]: long straights, two big jumps and banked hairpins.
[*][b]Crosswire[/b]: a neon figure-8 with a full loop.
[*][b]Null Gap[/b]: narrow, rail-less roads over the void.
[*][b]Switchback Spire[/b]: stacked switchbacks up to a summit, then a dive.
[*][b]Ringworld[/b]: a figure-8 among giant rings.
[*][b]Skyway[/b]: a stadium above the clouds with launch ramps.
[*][b]Helix[/b]: a spiral into the sky and a banked dive home.
[/list]

[h2]Tips[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shield[/b] is your health. [b]Charge[/b] is your boost, spins and ability.
[*]Fill charge by drafting behind cars, drifting, charge platforms, big jumps and the Pulse wave each lap.
[*]Hit jumps at full speed or you'll drop into space.
[/list]

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
[list]
[*]No plugins needed. The map sets its own cvars, and bots race with their own driving AI.
[*]If your server runs plugins, turn off AFK kickers, respawn / spawn-protection, teamkill punishment, round control, loadout, noblock and third-person plugins for this map.
[/list]
