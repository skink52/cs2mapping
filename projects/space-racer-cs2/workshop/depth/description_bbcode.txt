[h1]DEPTH RACER[/h1]
[b]32-car anti-grav racing on the sea floor, with giant sea creatures.[/b] Up to 64 players race at once on six original deep-sea tracks, with boost pads, jumps, glides, spin attacks and a special ability for every car. Watch out for the sea serpents, manta rays and whales crossing the track. The top 3 collect real CS2 kills. No plugins: the whole game runs inside the map.

[quote][b]Made mostly by AI.[/b] The code, art, sound and music were all generated by an AI (Claude, by Anthropic). skink52 directed, tested and published it.[/quote]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How a round works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pick a car[/b] in the lobby (A / D to choose, E to confirm).
[*][b]Line up on the grid.[/b] T and CT race together. With 33+ players there are two races on two tracks.
[*][b]Race 2–3 laps.[/b] The first 6 seconds after GO are a clean start: no damage and no attacks.
[*][b]The top 3 survive.[/b] Everyone else dies, and those kills go to the podium. Wreck someone yourself and that kill is yours.
[*][b]Your team wins the round[/b] by getting more cars into the top 8.
[/list]

[h2]Sea creatures[/h2]
Giant creatures cross the track over the jumps and glides, back and forth on a cycle.
[list]
[*]Get hit and you're shoved aside, skid and lose shield. Time your run, or go over or under them in a glide.
[*]A warning comes up before a crossing. Bastion's Aegis shell blocks them.
[/list]

[h2]Gliding[/h2]
On some tracks the road runs out and you glide through the water, through a corridor of glowing rings.
[list]
[*][b]S pulls up, W noses down[/b] (like a plane). A / D steer. You keep full thrust, and boost, spins and abilities all still work.
[*][b]Gold rings[/b] boost you. [b]Blue rings[/b] refill charge and shield.
[*]Hit the edge of the corridor and you bounce off it like a rail. At the end it narrows and puts you back on the road.
[/list]

[h2]Four cars[/h2]
[table]
[tr][th]Car[/th][th]Style[/th][th]Ability (E)[/th][/tr]
[tr][td][b]Hornet[/b][/td][td]Light and sharp. Drifting fills charge fast.[/td][td][b]Seeker Bolt:[/b] a homing shot that hits the first car ahead.[/td][/tr]

Every car's glowing trim shows its team: orange for T, blue for CT, gold for last round's winners.

[h2]Six tracks[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Kelp Canyon[/b]: a winding canyon through a kelp forest. A sea serpent swims through the chasm jump.
[*][b]Coral Crown[/b]: a stadium round a giant coral ring. Glide off the reef shelf under a manta ray.
[*][b]The Wreck[/b]: a figure-8 through and over a sunken liner, and a jump off the bow past a serpent.
[*][b]Vent Field[/b]: a rolling hexagon over hot vents. A whale lumbers across the second jump.
[*][b]Abyss Drop[/b]: spiral up a seamount, then glide down into the trench past a whale and a serpent.
[*][b]Lantern Deep[/b]: a dark loop with a rail-less ledge and a glide over a sinkhole.
[/list]

[h2]Tips[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shield[/b] is your health. [b]Charge[/b] is your boost, spins and ability.
[*]Fill charge by drafting behind cars, drifting, charge pads and rings, big jumps and the Pulse wave each lap.
[*]Hit jumps at full speed or you'll drop into the deep. In a glide, line up early for the next ring. Watch the creatures' timing before you commit.
[/list]

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
[list]
[*]No plugins needed. The map sets its own cvars, and bots race, glide and dodge creatures with their own driving AI.
[*]If your server runs plugins, turn off AFK kickers, respawn / spawn-protection, teamkill punishment, round control, loadout, noblock and third-person plugins for this map.
[/list]
