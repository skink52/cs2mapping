[h1]Hellmatch ZE[/h1]
Zombie escape inside one CS2 map, in the retro 90s look of Hellmatch. T and CT each run their own copy of the same Freedoom level at the same time: hold zones while gates open, kill the level's guardian, then hold the exit. Every human who dies rises as a demon in the [i]other[/i] team's copy and hunts them alongside the level's monsters. The first team to get out wins. No plugins: the whole game is the map's own script. To try it, subscribe and start it from Play > Workshop Maps with some bots (bot_quota 16).

[h2]How a round goes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Both teams start as humans at the start of their copy of the level (pistol and shotgun; the level's weapons and pickups are there to grab).
[*]Stages run in order and the HUD shows the current one: [b]HOLD[/b] stages count down while at least one human stands in the zone (the timer pauses, not resets, when nobody is in it); a gate opens when the hold is done. A [b]BOSS[/b] stage spawns the level's guardian, with more health the more humans are alive. The last stage is the [b]ESCAPE[/b]: hold the exit to the end.
[*]Side routes are shut, so the way on is the way the stages open. Demons and monsters keep coming from behind.
[*]Die and you rise as a demon in the other team's level: an imp (fireball), a flesh worm (lunge) or a deadflare (dash). Demons respawn after a few seconds behind the humans. Every human killed by a demon player is a real CS2 kill for that player.
[/list]

[h2]Winning[/h2]
[list]
[*]A team that finishes the escape stage with a human in the exit wins at once.
[*]A team with no humans left loses; if both are wiped on the same tick it is a draw.
[*]At the time limit (9 to 14 minutes) the team with more humans alive wins, then the team further along the stages.
[/list]

[h2]Levels[/h2]
A different level each round until all have been played:
[list]
[*][b]Hydroelectric Plant[/b] (MAP01): hold the floodgate and the pump station, defeat the pain lord, escape through the flooded cave.
[*][b]Outer Prison[/b] (E1M1): hold the atrium gate and the flooded hall, slay the combat slug, drop into the pit and run to the exit.
[*][b]Land of the Lost[/b] (E3M1): hold the stairs and the temple plaza, kill the technospider, run south to the exit gate.
[*][b]Janus Terminal[/b] (MAP06): hold the east gate and the pillar hall, destroy the assault tripod, run back to the exit.
[*][b]Supply Depot[/b] (E1M4, long): two holds, a pain bringer in the sludge pit, hold the nukage walkway, a pain lord, run north.
[*][b]Crude Processing Center[/b] (MAP03, long): two holds, a dark soldier among the pillars, hold the crossroads, an assault tripod in the core, hold the exit lift.
[/list]
Weapons upgrade as the stages fall, and every finished hold refills ammo and health.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
As a human: W A S D move (always running), Shift walk, mouse aim, Mouse 1 fire, E or Space use (doors, switches), R or Mouse 2 next weapon, F previous weapon.
As a demon: Mouse 1 claw / bite, [b]Mouse 2 or R: your ability[/b] (imp fireball, flesh worm lunge, deadflare dash; the HUD shows when it is ready).
While rising: R / Mouse 2 next class, F previous. Boss stage: press E in the first 5 s to become the boss yourself.
Music volume: the [b]Main Menu Music[/b] slider (snd_menumusic_volume).
Chat commands (! or /): [b]!help[/b] controls, [b]!hud[/b] status bar size, [b]!spec[/b] watch, [b]!afk[/b] sit out, [b]!back[/b] rejoin.

[h2]Server notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Map: mg_hellescape, game type Custom. Load it by Workshop ID: host_workshop_map <this item's ID>.
[*]Nothing to install. The map sets its own round rules (no buying or money, freeze time 0, its own round time; respawns are handled by the script). Set APPLY_CVARS to false in maps/scripts/hellescape.js to keep your own config.
[*]Plugins that get in the way: AFK managers, no-respawn plugins, spawn protection, teamkill punishment and anything that blocks scripted damage (kills are applied through the map script so the killfeed and scoreboard work).
[*]CS2 bots play (bot_quota fills empty slots); up to 64 players.
[*]The console prints "script time" once a minute. SERVER_STATS in the script's settings sends stats to a server stats plugin (mgstat commands; off by default).
[*]Early build. Please report problems in the discussions.
[/list]

[h2]How it was made[/h2]
The code (map script, level converter, stage files, build tools) and most of the engineering were done with an AI coding assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), directed, tested and tuned by skink52. Hellmatch ZE is built on Hellmatch and reuses its code and art. The four "GEN" music tracks were generated for Hellmatch (original, CC0). Everything else you see and hear is Freedoom's work, under its BSD licence (below). Source code: https://github.com/skink52/cs2mapping/tree/main/projects/hellmatch-ze-cs2

Hellmatch ZE is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Freedoom project, id Software, Bethesda or Valve; Freedoom is named only to credit its content.
