# Workshop change notes (as posted on Steam)

Newest first. Each entry is the text pasted into the Workshop item's change notes when that build is uploaded.

## First release (not yet uploaded)
- Hellmatch ZE: zombie escape in one CS2 map. T and CT each run their own copy of the same Freedoom level; hold the zones,
  kill the guardian, hold the exit.
- Escape finale: the team that escapes is teleported into a small deathmatch arena (FreeDM's Issues of Claveria) with its
  weapons. If the other team is wiped, the escaped team wins; if it escapes too, it follows them in and T fights CT there
  (a finale death is final) until one team is left.
- Six levels: Hydroelectric Plant (MAP01, pain lord), Outer Prison (E1M1, combat slug), Land of the Lost (E3M1,
  technospider) and Janus Terminal (MAP06, assault tripod) with four stages each, and two long ones with six: Supply Depot
  (E1M4: pain bringer, then a pain lord over the nukage) and Crude Processing Center (MAP03: dark soldier, then an assault
  tripod in the core). A different level each round until all have been played.
- Every finished hold refills ammo and health; the stages hand out weapon upgrades; demons come in surges.
- Die and you rise as a demon in the other team's level: imp (fireball), flesh worm (lunge) or deadflare (dash), on
  Mouse 2 or R. Kills on enemy humans are real CS2 kills.
- Bots play both sides: human bots follow the stages, demon bots chase.
- Music: the Hellmatch theme, each level's own Freedoom track, Hellmatch's four generated tracks, and a Freedoom boss
  track for every guardian.
- One loading screen per level with its stages and map.
- Fixes from the first playtest: the lost soul (deadflare) floats like the monster does; demons respawn only where they
  can reach the humans and get through doors on their way (a few demon spawns behind press-E doors moved or their doors
  opened by the stages); the red damage flash is softer; demon players pass through each other and through monster
  demons, so packs don't jam (still solid to humans and shootable).
