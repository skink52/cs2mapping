## Review
## Review (6 Nov 2026)

Source: `browse.py` (new): 41 Workshop browse pages (top rated 7/30/90/365 days, "playermodel" top rated, trending, most subscribed),
587 items not in the catalog, 503 that look like player models. 57 picked for a scan (human-sized adults, mostly under 250 MB); 32
converted, 2 dropped after review, 30 kept. Reviewed the contact sheet, the `_posed` pictures and both hands montages.

- **Dropped:** Batman (Arkham City), whose cape hangs off its own bones: CS2 has none, so it stays at its bind position and stretches into
  a sheet behind him. Saints Row IV Ronin, whose left hand flattens into a ribbon in CS2's pose.
- **Swapped:** Agent Smith first came from `smithy_pon_pm`, the "MegaSmith" boss (a patchwork of Smiths); now the plain suit.
- **Kept with a note:** Postal Dude is black and white by design (noir), and his `Outline` mesh is an inverted hull: back faces only, so
  CS2 culls its front like Source does. Walter White's rig has no index-finger chain: the hands aren't aligned (warned); the fingers stay
  open in CS2's pose but don't break. Evrart Claire is very round: bulky-torso warning, the right hand sinks into the belly on rifles.
- **Warnings:** 12 of 30, mostly armour or coats (bulky torso). The NBC hazmat suit and the NCR ranger also have legs 8-9 u off CS2's
  joints (long-coat or suit proportions); their knees bend a little off.
- **Server test (slime-mg-gm `tests/run.py --gmod-models`):** all 80 models (batches 1-4) worn by bots that fought on ar_shoots: 80/80 ok
  (worn, hits registered by hitgroup, no console or plugin errors). Hit spread over all models head 7.6 %, chest 21.5, stomach 35.7,
  arms 8.5, legs 26.5, against 9.0 / 24.1 / 34.7 / 9.8 / 22.0 for the stock-agent control bots in the same fights.

Toolkit changes this batch: `browse.py`; the catalog and the tools read JSON as UTF-8 and print Workshop titles in UTF-8 (a Thai
character in a title stopped the first scan); titles are stored on one line (some carry newlines that broke the tables);
`pipeline.py` / `fetch.py` take `--only a,b` as well as `--only=a,b` (the usage line showed the first, the code read only the second,
so a one-model re-run converted every model).
