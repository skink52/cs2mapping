## Review (6 Nov 2026)

Source: the 90-day "playermodel" search, pages 2-3. 24 items downloaded (one retried after SteamCMD answered "Busy"), `--suggest` passed 15;
4 more passed but were ultra-low-poly (406-736 vertices, PS1/HL1 style: Mr. Bean, James Doakes, Jerma, HUNK) and were skipped. 20 models
from 12 items (packs: BF4 x2, Urban Assault x5, SCP:CB x3, MGS5 x2). Reviewed the contact sheet, every `_posed` picture and both hands montages.

Problems found and fixed in the toolkit (all models re-run after each fix):

- **Duplicate VMT keys.** Michael Scott's face came out blue: his VMT lists `$baseTexture` twice, the second time pointing at the normal map.
  Source keeps the first value; the VMT reader kept the last. Now keeps the first (55 VMTs in the downloads had duplicate keys; two of them,
  Michael Scott and ERRORMAN's cape, changed the result).
- **Bodygroup defaults.** SCP Class-D and the SCP guard wore party hats and props, others extra gear: the converter took the first body part
  option *with geometry*, skipping a blank option 0. The game's default is option 0 (blank = part off). Now option 0 unless the catalog's
  `bodygroups` says otherwise; 11 models in batches 1-3 changed (e.g. the CS:S SWAT now comes without vest and gear, the MGS5 soldiers without
  hat and gas mask, Gordon Restored without ponytail). Add gear back per model with `"bodygroups": {"vest": 1}`.
- **Riot shield.** The Urban Assault Falcon commander's option 0 *is* a riot shield (white blob on the arm); switched off in the catalog
  (`"bodygroups": {"Riot Shield": 1}`).
- **Legacy `.bin` downloads** without an LZMA end marker failed to extract (Picolas Cage); `gma.py` now decompresses them as a stream.
- **Cleanup during a run.** `cleanup.py` deleted the SteamCMD copies while the batch ran, and `fetch.extract` looked for the download before
  the extract: 18 models failed with "nothing downloaded" and were re-run. `fetch.extract` now uses an existing extract first.
- The compile-log filter flagged ERRORMAN's own file names as errors; it now matches only real error/warning lines.
- **3-finger rig** (SCP Class-D: thumb, index, middle only): hand alignment needed index + pinky and silently skipped the hand; now uses the
  outermost finger the rig has, and warns if a hand still can't be aligned.
- **Forearm ribbon** (SCP Class-D): a two-ring forearm flattened into a blade when CS2 rolled it. New twist split (limb triangles split where the
  twist changes sharply; 270 triangles on Class-D) and hard-skinned joint softening. All 50 models re-run with these.

Looks: Steamhappy's emoji head and Picolas Cage's pickle body are the source designs. Leva (Girls Frontline 2) is the slimmest so far (scale
1.14 limit); her thin fingers read spiky when bent but follow their bones.
